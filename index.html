FAQ

Questions?

When do I pay?

Payment terms are confirmed with the team before an order is started. Final details and payment are confirmed through your Discord ticket.

Can prices be negotiated?

Yes. Calculator amounts are starting quotes. If a quote doesn't work for you, contact ROCKET LEAGUE MARKED through Discord and ask about a custom price.

What is the difference between Solo Boost and Duo Boost?

Solo Boost means a ROCKET LEAGUE MARKED booster plays on your account at the standard quote. Duo Boost means you play together with our booster and is charged at 2× the standard quote.

Can I request a screenshare?

Yes. Ask the team in your Discord ticket and screenshare can be arranged where appropriate.

What payment methods do you accept?

Discord Nitro, PayPal, and Robux.

Where can I buy accounts?

Open the ROCKET LEAGUE MARKED Discord and ask the team about current availability.

Which platforms do you support?

The website currently accepts PC, PlayStation and Xbox requests. Confirm any platform-specific requirements with the team in your Discord ticket.

How long will my order take?

Estimated delivery depends on the service and team availability. The team will confirm an estimate in your Discord ticket before the service begins.

How do I place an order?

Choose your service and options, press the order button, then paste the prepared request into your ROCKET LEAGUE MARKED Discord ticket.

Are calculator prices final?

Regular boosting calculator amounts are starting quotes. Tournament, coaching and custom service prices are confirmed by the team in Discord before you proceed.
